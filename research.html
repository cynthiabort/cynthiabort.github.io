2026Preprint

Depth Separation for Neural Networks under Gaussian Measure

Cynthia Bortolotto, Francisco Ganacim, and Roberto I. Oliveira

arXiv:2610.28800

arXiv ↗PDF ↗
About this paper

A radial function under the standard Gaussian measure that depth-3 ReLU networks of polynomial size approximate well, but depth-2 networks with bounded weights only at exponential size. The key tool is a lower bound for functions with substantial mass in high Hermite frequencies. All results are verified in Lean 4.

2026Preprint

A complex-analytic proof of square-restricted stable phase retrieval in Fock space

Cynthia Bortolotto and João P. G. Ramos

arXiv:2608.26365

arXiv ↗PDF ↗
About this paper

A complex-analytic approach to local stability near the Gaussian in Fock space. The proof is also verified in Lean with assistance from large language models.

2026Preprint

Modular Forms and Numerical Explorations of Rational Approximations to ζ(3)

Cynthia Bortolotto and Lucas Oliveira

arXiv:2605.00673

arXiv ↗PDF ↗
About this paper

We investigate a family of auxiliary modular forms in Beukers’ irrationality argument for ζ(3), and explore related constructions for other genus-zero Fricke groups.
