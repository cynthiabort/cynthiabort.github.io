About me

Analysis, learning & Lean

I work in mathematical analysis and machine learning theory, with interests in approximation theory and the mathematical foundations of neural networks.

I am also interested in formalizing mathematics in Lean and in making AI-assisted mathematical research more accessible.

I received my Ph.D. from ETH Zürich in 2024, under the supervision of Emmanuel Kowalski and Özlem Imamoglu. I am now based at the Instituto de Matemática Pura e Aplicada in Rio de Janeiro.

  • Mathematical analysis
  • Machine learning theory
  • Approximation theory
  • Foundations of neural networks
  • Formalization in Lean
  • Analytic number theory
